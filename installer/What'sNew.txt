NLSI Exclusive Logbook v1.3.1-alpha has been installed.

WHAT'S NEW

- Updated the native Qt 6 interface with official NLSI branding.
- Simplified navigation to Dashboard, Live Drive, Jobs, History, Events,
  Settings, and About.
- Grouped provider and telemetry diagnostics under Settings.
- Kept telemetry values and history status truthful when information is
  unavailable.

The SCS plugin supplies the live telemetry fields available to the application.
Native RenCloud ingestion and persisted history are not available in this build.
