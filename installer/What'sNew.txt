- Preserved user logs, events, sessions, jobs, and offline operation.
- Live ETS2/ATS telemetry and actual game-folder installation have not been tested for this release.

WHAT'S NEW

- Added Asia/Manila date/time with milliseconds, TruckSim cruise-control and retarder details when available, and application-version/About links.
- Preserved the 900x600 minimum/default window size and responsive Qt layout.
